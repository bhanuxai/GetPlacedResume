4 Simple Steps to Make Your Resume 100% ATS Compliant

  1. Use a Single-Column Linear Format: Avoid tables, multi-column designs, graphics, and floating text boxes. Modern ATS parsers read left-to-right, top-to-bottom; multi-column layouts frequently scramble work experience chronologies.
  2. Incorporate Exact Job Description Keywords: Mirror the exact terminology from the target job posting. If the employer asks for "Project Management", "React.js", or "CI/CD pipelines", include those exact keywords naturally in your skills section and bullet points.
  3. Quantify Accomplishments with the Google XYZ Formula: Frame every accomplishment as: "Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]." For instance: "Reduced API response times by 42% (Y) across 3 core microservices (X) by implementing Redis caching and query indexing (Z)."
  4. Run a Pre-Submission ATS Resume Scan: Use GetPlacedResume to test your resume parseability, check your match score, and eliminate any hidden formatting errors before applying.

What is an ATS resume checker?

An ATS resume checker is software that replicates how recruitment parsers process job applications. It analyzes text readability, extracts key attributes with Named Entity Recognition, evaluates keyword similarity against job postings, and checks layout structure for ATS safety.

What is a good resume ATS score?

A score of 80% or higher is considered competitive and places your resume in the top percentile of applicants. Scores between 70% and 79% are good but have room for keyword improvement. A score below 60% indicates critical formatting traps or missing job description keywords.

Why do 75% of resumes get rejected by ATS before a recruiter sees them?

Most resumes are filtered out due to incompatible multi-column formatting, missing primary hard skills, unquantified bullet points, or contact information trapped inside headers and footers that parsers discard.

What is the best resume format to pass ATS filters in 2026?

The standard reverse-chronological single-column resume format is universally preferred by all ATS engines. Use standard fonts, clear headings ('Work Experience', 'Education', 'Skills'), and submit in standard PDF or DOCX format.
